Introduction

Russell and Norvig (2020) define AI as "the study of agents that receive percepts from the environment and perform actions." The term "artificial intelligence" was coined in 1956 by John McCarthy, who defined it as "the science and engineering of making intelligent machines." AI has evolved significantly since its inception, with advancements in machine learning, deep learning, and natural language processing driving its growth. Today, AI is applied in various domains, including healthcare, finance, transportation, and entertainment, transforming industries and society at large.
